## Intended usage and derivative assessment

Future intended usage: Android, iOS, website, and admin portal.

The square raster is suitable as the preserved visual reference for Android/iOS
asset preparation. Its opaque background, baked-in wordmark/tagline, fine lines,
and shaded detail make the full composition unsuitable for direct use at small
launcher sizes: text and detail will become illegible.

- Small Android/iOS launcher icons need separately approved simplified artwork
  and platform-sized exports, with padding checked at actual display sizes.
- Android adaptive icons need separate foreground/background preparation and
  mask-safe placement; this opaque JPEG does not supply isolated layers.
- Splash screens need separate optimized exports and layout/background checks
  on Android and iOS. Whether to retain the complete wordmark and tagline depends
  on their legibility at the chosen display size.
- Website/admin usage may need optimized responsive exports, and transparent
  variants if the surrounding layout requires them.

No derivatives were generated or wired into the app by this registration.
Platform requirements must be checked against the project's Expo SDK and current
platform documentation when those derivatives are implemented. Prefer an
owner-supplied vector or layered master for that future work if available; avoid
upscaling this JPEG as a substitute for additional source detail.
